# Nemade Trait Prediction - Thesis Log

In this file, I will keep track of my progress, link to relevant papers, and share my thoughts on a daily basis as my project moves along.

## Project Description

Coral reefs harbour a very high diversity of nematodes, but most species remain unstudied.
This limits our ability to understand ecological patterns and use them for ecosystem monitoring.
This project uses multimodal machine learning based on a phylogenetic tree (GraphN), DNA (sequences) to predict categorical traits for poorly known species, making coral reef nematode communities usable for ecological research and biomonitoring.

Supervisors: Rita Pucci (LIACS & Naturalis), Jan Macher (Naturalis & CML), Marco Visser (CML)
